# Contributing to Ektypos

Thank you for helping. A few rules keep the project's licensing clean.

## License of contributions

Ektypos is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. By contributing, you agree that
your contribution is licensed under the same terms. You keep your copyright;
the project is collectively "The Ektypos authors".

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Every commit must be signed off, which certifies the Developer Certificate of
Origin 1.1 (https://developercertificate.org/): that you wrote the change, or
have the right to submit it under the project's license.

    git commit -s -m "Your message"

This adds a line such as `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>`.

## Third-party material

- Do not paste code, fonts, icons or images from other projects without
  saying so in the pull request.
- Anything added must be under a license compatible with AGPL-3.0 (for
  example MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0, ISC, CC BY 4.0, SIL OFL 1.1) and must be
  listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md with its copyright line and license.
- Do not add material extracted from the vendor's Android app or firmware.
  Protocol facts learned by analysis are fine; copied code or artwork is not.
- Do not add brand logos.
